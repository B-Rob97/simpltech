# Theme photography

Created using the built-in imagegen tool. These are conceptual studio images, not photographs of SimplTech's office or client projects. The source PNGs were converted to WebP using Sharp for local delivery.

## mission-orbit.webp

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool and saved here at 1800 × 1200 (107,652 bytes). This is a fictional cinematic planet, not a scientific image.

Prompt: Cinematic fine-art space photograph, landscape 3:2, for a premium digital studio website. A huge dark planet viewed very close from orbit occupies the right two thirds and lower frame, upper curved horizon with thin warm ivory atmospheric rim, sun just beyond horizon at upper right casting a small intense amber glow. Planet surface midnight charcoal with subtle intricate topography and cloud swirls, no recognizable earth continents, almost monochrome, extraordinarily realistic NASA large format orbital photography, subtle analog film grain, deep black negative space across left third. Elegant, serene, immense scale, high contrast yet soft halation. No text, no labels, no UI, no spacecraft, no logos, no purple or blue nebula. Standalone full bleed photograph, not a website mockup.
